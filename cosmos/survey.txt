How to compute planetary positions


The formula for calculating the tidal height (h) at a given location (latitude ϕ and longitude λ) and time (t) based on the positions of the moon and sun is as follows:

h = A_m * cos(2π * (t - t_m) / T_m) * cos(2π * δ_m / 360) * cos(2π * (λ - λ_m) / 360) + A_s * cos(2π * (t - t_s) / T_s) * cos(2π * δ_s / 360) * cos(2π * (λ - λ_s) / 360)

Where:

    A_m and A_s are the amplitudes of the tidal forces due to the moon and sun, respectively (A_m ≈ 0.42 m, A_s ≈ 0.23 m)
    t is the time for which the tidal height is calculated
    t_m and t_s are the times of the moon's and sun's meridian transits (when they cross the local meridian), respectively
    T_m and T_s are the periods of the moon's and sun's orbits around the Earth (T_m ≈ 24 hours 50 minutes, T_s ≈ 24 hours)
    δ_m and δ_s are the declinations (angular distances north or south of the celestial equator) of the moon and sun, respectively
    λ is the longitude of the location
    λ_m and λ_s are the longitudes of the moon and sun, respectively
