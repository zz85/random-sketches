Brush runs dry

Real watercolour: paint is pigment carried by water. It flows while wet, pools at the edges as it dries, settles into the paper's grain, and glazes over dried paint. While the dot by the drop button pulses, the sheet is wet.

Paintbrushes on the left (tap the selected one again for its settings), size / water / paint sliders next to them, pigments along the bottom
Colourstap a pigment well; + Mix (or long-press a well) puts several on the brush, and they mix like paint: blue + yellow = green. The rainbow well or the big round well opens the colour wheel (ring = hue, inside = shade)
Pen modePen | Paint at the top. Pen writes ink straight onto the page, with no water: ballpoint, fineliner, brush pen, highlighter, eraser along the bottom, ink colours next to them, + for any colour. Gallery → + → Notebook page gives lined or grid paper
Pencil / styluspaints; pressure sets size and load, tilt lays the brush on its side
