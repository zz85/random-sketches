Oxide: Codevember, Rust, third take.

Close-up landscapes of rust on old turquoise paint, the few centimetres a macro lens sees: tide lines, crazed plates, orange powder and lacy islands of glossy paint. Every pixel's ray is marched through a height field that TSL compute shaders build around the camera in nested levels, so the detail follows the screen and the steel runs to the horizon. Leave it alone and the camera glides over it like a lunar orbiter.

three.js r187, WebGPU, TSL. Works offline.
